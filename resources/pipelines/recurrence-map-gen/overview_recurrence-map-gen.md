### Glioblastoma Recurrence Map Pipeline

ML models trained on a multi-institute dataset of high-grade gliomas estimate spatial patterns of tumor infiltration and predict locations of future recurrence, allowing for personalized, precision targeting.

Input: Conventional preoperative MRI scans (T1-weighted, T1-post contrast, T2-weighted, T2-Flair), Diffusion preoperative MRI (either Apparent Diffusion Coefficient, or Repetition Time). 

Output: 3-D Recurrence probability map (Nifti)
